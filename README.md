# Good Business

**Good Business** is a values-based discovery application designed to help consumers make more informed decisions about where they spend their money.

Users can search and filter businesses based on priorities like local ownership, sustainability, community involvement, and more, via credibally sourced information from the team at **Good Business**. 

## Critical User Flows / Pages
### Home
The **Home** page was added to provide a better landing page for users. The tone of this page offers a clean layout with clear callouts to what makes **Good Business** what it is. Users can navigate to different pages via the navbar here or also access the **How it Works** or **Suggest** pages in the CTA at the bottom.

### Explore
Navigating to the **Explore** page will allow the user to filter through priorities that they value and view relevant business profiles. Each business card has the business name, type of business, priority matches, and a short description of the business. 

Users can view the business details further on individualized business pages when selecting the **View Business** buttons.

Users finally have the opportunity to suggest a business at the bottom of the page, in case they don't already see a business they'd like to explore.

### How It Works
The **How It Works** page explains **Good Business's** methodology in three simple steps, what the transparency labels mean, and gives the user options to go back to the **Explore** or **Suggest a Business** pages - keeping the user within the **Good Business** website flow.

### Suggest a Business
The **Suggest** page hosts a suggestion form for users to be able to suggest a new business that they don't already see on **Good Business**. Users can submit a business, understand the importance of suggesting a new business, and navigate back the **How It Works** page to learn more.

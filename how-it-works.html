How Good Business Works

We show where business information comes from so you can decide what matters to you.

1. Choose Your Priorities

Select the business values that matter most to you, such as sustainability, ownership, community involvement, and accessibility.

2. Explore Businesses

Browse businesses and compare information based on the priorities you selected.

3. Check the Source

See where each claim came from and whether the information has been verified, reported by the business, or is currently unknown.

What do the labels mean?

Verified

Information confirmed through an independent or publicly available source.

Business Reported

Information provided or published directly by the business.

Third-Party Resource

Information provided or published from a third-party source that has not been fully verified.

Unavailable

Reliable information is not currently available.

How Good Business Works

Good Business helps you discover businesses based on the values and practices that matter most to you.

Finding a Good Business Starts With You.

Explore businesses in three simple steps. You decide which priorities matter most, and we'll help you find businesses that reflect them.

01

Choose Your Priorities

Select the business practices and values that are important to you, such as sustainability, local ownership, accessibility, or community involvement.

02

Explore Businesses

Browse businesses and see which of your selected priorities align with the information available about each business.

03

Make Informed Choices

Review business details, explore supporting information, and decide which businesses you want to support.

Transparency Matters.

Not all business information comes from the same source. Good Business uses clear labels to help you understand where information comes from and what has been confirmed.

Verified

Verified Information

Information reviewed and confirmed through an available supporting source.

Business Reported

Business-Provided Information

Information shared directly by a business that has not yet been independently confirmed.

Third-Party Resource

External Information

Information linked to external resources for additional context and research.

Unavailable

Information Not Available

Information that is not currently available or has not been provided.

A missing label or unavailable information does not necessarily reflect a business's practices or values.
